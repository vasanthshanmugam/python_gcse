        AFTER THE FREE TASTER

          Lots of runs before finishing is normal and healthy: it means your child is experimenting. These levels are a guide to how much help was needed. They are not a GCSE grade or a prediction of one. Progress is saved to your child's account, so it follows them to any computer they log in on.

          World 1 of Code Quarry is a free taster, with no ads or tracking. A login is needed to play. We store the parent's email address and, for each child, a username, their progress including the code they write in quests, and any school and year group a parent adds. If a parent opts in, a child's generated nickname and points appear on leaderboards that only logged-in players can see. Payments are handled by PayPal, and we never see card or bank details. The Python engine and fonts download from jsDelivr and Google Fonts. Topics follow the GCSE Computer Science specifications (OCR J277 and AQA 8525), but the game isn't endorsed by either exam board.
